# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + React + Tailwind CSS + Motion. User-specified explicitly in the brief.

## Users

[Inferred — demo project, no real client to interview.] Affluent homeowners across the Cotswolds and neighbouring counties (Gloucestershire, Oxfordshire, Wiltshire) who own period country houses, rectories, farmhouses, or barn conversions with significant garden or land. They are considering a garden design commission — either a full redesign after buying/renovating a property, or a phased renovation of an inherited or tired garden. They are visiting the site to judge whether the studio's taste and track record match the scale and character of their own property before requesting a consultation.

## Product Purpose

Hartley & Sedge is a boutique garden and landscape design studio. The website exists to demonstrate design authority through real-feeling portfolio storytelling, explain the studio's process clearly enough to lower the anxiety of a high-value commission, and convert qualified visitors into enquiry calls.

## Positioning

Designs gardens that read as though they have always belonged to the house and land they surround — structured enough to hold a formal country house, loose and naturalistic enough to feel unforced. Rooted in site-specific planting ecology rather than a repeatable "look," in the lineage of English naturalistic design (Stuart-Smith/Pearson-adjacent) rather than manicured formality or trend-led minimalism.

## Operating Context

- Commission process: Consultation → Concept & Masterplan → Planting Palette → Build Oversight → Aftercare/Seasonal Care.
- Projects range from single-border renovations to multi-acre masterplans; typical commission size implies a considered, unhurried sales process (this is not an e-commerce or instant-quote business).
- Studio is small and founder-led, not a large contracting firm — credibility comes from taste, portfolio depth, and press/awards, not scale.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- This is a demo/portfolio project: the business, its people, testimonials, press mentions, and awards are invented for demonstration purposes and are explicitly fictional, not real claims about a real company.
- No backend/CMS required; content is static. The enquiry form should be a real, functioning UI (validated, with success/error states) but does not need a live email backend for this demo.
- Imagery: no real client photography exists; use high-quality, license-appropriate stock photography curated for a single consistent visual world (warm, naturalistic English garden photography), not generic corporate stock.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Hartley & Sedge.
- Founder: Eleanor Hartley (fictional), RHS-trained garden designer, founded the studio in 2011.
- Based in Cirencester, Gloucestershire.
- Tone of voice: quietly confident, editorial, unhurried — closer to a well-written magazine profile than marketing copy. No exclamation marks, no "unlock/elevate/seamless" SaaS vocabulary.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. All portfolio projects, testimonials, press mentions, and awards referenced in the site are invented to be plausible and specific (never generic), and are clearly a demonstration, not a factual claim.

## Product Principles

1. Restraint reads as expertise — the site should feel edited, not decorated; every element must earn its place.
2. Photography carries the argument. Copy supports and frames images; it never competes with them.
3. Motion should feel like turning pages in a well-made book — unhurried, purposeful, never showy or SaaS-generic (no bouncy card tilts, no gradient blobs, no glassmorphism).
4. The site must feel bespoke to this studio specifically, never like a template a garden business happened to fill in.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No client-specified requirement; build to standard WCAG AA practice (contrast, focus states, reduced-motion support) as baseline craft, not a stated constraint.
