## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a boutique estate guidebook to the studio's own work — the small hardback/paperback National Trust-style guide sold at a great garden's gate — not another glossy landscaping portfolio with a hero photo and service cards. It refuses the category default (stock-photo hero + icon-card services + carousel testimonials) and the "opposite" minimalist white gallery.

OWN-WORLD: Deep bracken-green cloth-cover ground carries 40–50% of the surface (nav rail, section dividers, footer) — never a cream page-ground default. Content panels sit on warm buff/oat paper, framed with a fine keyline like a tipped-in plate. Ink is near-black bracken-brown, not pure black. One reserved accent — muted quarry/brick red, drawn from Ordnance Survey footpath convention — marks only the trail line, active stop, and the enquiry CTA. Type: Zilla Slab (display/numerals, collision scale for the hero line), Spectral (book-serif narrative body + italic captions), Archivo (tracked-caps wayfinding labels). No italic-serif-display-on-cream default.

STORY: A visitor is deciding whether this studio's taste matches their own property. They land inside "Room No. 1," scroll a numbered trail through the studio's world (Gardens/portfolio, The Trail/process, The Founder/about, In Print/press, Visit/enquiry), and leave believing the studio designs with restraint and site-specific ecology, ready to enquire.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed misty parkland photograph as "Plate No. 1." A green cloth-texture band crosses the top holding the wordmark + small ampersand mark and the numbered nav (dimmed/ghosted for unvisited stops, per the seven-segment raise below). Collision-scale Zilla Slab headline sits on a solid buff caption-card (fine keyline, no glassmorphism) over the lower third of the photo. A brick-red "No. 1" plate numeral sits beside it. Below the fold, a horizontal fold-out plan strip previews the trail's remaining stops as an estate-map nav rail.

FORM: Estate-guidebook-trail direction, index 6 of 7 grounded candidates (masterplan/cartographic, botanical plate, gardener's ledger, architect's drawing set, seed catalogue, **estate guidebook**, signage system), seed key 41826033. Raises taken from the dealt challengers (all declined on audience/product-clarity grounds, each still donating one discipline): from the bioluminescent-wake challenger, a soft fading scroll-trail line traced along the nav rail as the visitor progresses; from the brick-instruction-book challenger, the Process section built as a numbered exploded diagram with call-out arrows, not icon cards; from the boxing-poster challenger, the hero headline commits to true collision scale with supporting trust marks ranked by descending type size alone, no logo row; from the rubber-hose-cel challenger, section transitions use a soft oval iris-wipe as the signature scroll interaction; from the HyperCard-stack challenger, every trail stop is a directly addressable, clickable pin on the plan nav; from the seven-segment challenger, unvisited nav stops are deliberately ghosted/dimmed ink so the active stop's contrast is a designed opposition, not a default state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
